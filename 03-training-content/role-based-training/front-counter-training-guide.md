# Front Counter Training Guide

This guide covers everything a Front Counter Associate needs to know to serve customers confidently and handle common situations correctly.

---

## Your Role

As a Front Counter Associate, you are the first point of contact for every customer who walks into Grumble & Crumb. Your responsibilities are:

- Greeting and serving customers.
- Processing sales in CrumbKeeper.
- Handling order pickups for custom orders.
- Performing allergen and charm disclosure.
- Processing refunds within your authority.
- Recognizing when to escalate.

---

## Section 1: Customer Greeting and Service Flow

### Standard Service Flow

1. Acknowledge the customer within 30 seconds of entry.
2. Ask how you can help.
3. For product questions: use product labels and the allergen/charm disclosure card.
4. Ring up the order in CrumbKeeper. For enchanted items, confirm charm disclosure before completing checkout.
5. Hand over the product with a charm-disclosure card in the bag.
6. Close warmly.

### If the Customer Has a Question You Can't Answer

- Say: *"That's a great question — let me check that for you."*
- Consult the operations manual or the Shift Lead. Do not guess.

---

## Section 2: Allergen and Charm Disclosure

You must disclose allergens and enchantment types before completing any sale of an enchanted item.

**Steps:**
1. Identify whether the item is enchanted (check the product label).
2. Describe the enchantment type to the customer: *"This is a whispering sourdough — it has a low-level auditory enchantment and will murmur softly for up to 24 hours."*
3. Confirm they have no concerns before completing the sale.
4. Place a charm-disclosure card in the bag.
5. Tap **Charm Disclosure Confirmed** in CrumbKeeper.

Full policy: [`allergen-and-charm-disclosure-policy.md`](../../01-business-units/bakery-operations/policies/allergen-and-charm-disclosure-policy.md)

---

## Section 3: Custom Order Pickup

1. Customer provides their name or order number.
2. Look up the order in Order Oracle.
3. Confirm the order is in **Ready for Pickup** status.
4. Retrieve the order from the custom order pickup shelf.
5. Confirm the customer's name and order details before handing over.
6. For singing cakes: demonstrate that the melody works by lighting a test candle briefly (if available).
7. Update Order Oracle status to **Fulfilled**.

---

## Section 4: Handling Complaints

Most complaints fall into one of these categories. Use the correct script.

| Complaint Type | Script Location |
|---|---|
| Wrong item | Scenario 1 in [`customer-apology-scripts.md`](../../01-business-units/bakery-operations/job-aids/customer-apology-scripts.md) |
| Singing cake wrong song | Scenario 2 |
| Enchantment faded | Scenario 3 |
| Overactive pastry reached customer | Scenario 4 — **Escalate immediately** |
| Custom order not ready | Scenario 5 |

For any complaint that requires a remake: confirm the item can be produced today and the customer is willing to wait. Then notify the Shift Lead or baker.

---

## Section 5: Processing Refunds

- Refunds up to **20 gold coins**: you handle independently.
- Refunds **20–100 gold coins**: call the Shift Lead to the register.
- Refunds **over 100 gold coins**: Shift Lead + Bakery Manager.

Always select a Refund Reason Code. Do not leave that field blank.

Full procedure: [`process-customer-refund.md`](../../01-business-units/bakery-operations/procedures/process-customer-refund.md)

## Section 7: When to Call the Shift Lead

Call the Shift Lead when:
- Any refund exceeds 20 gold coins.
- A customer reports harm or injury.
- A customer is verbally abusive or threatening.
- You cannot find an order in any system.
- A quarantined pastry is in the display area.
- Any situation you are not sure how to handle.

**When in doubt, call the Shift Lead. That is always the right move.**
