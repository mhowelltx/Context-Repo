# Apprentice Baker Training Guide

This guide covers the core knowledge and skills required for the Apprentice Baker role in the Grumble & Crumb back-of-house production area.

---

## Your Role

As an Apprentice Baker, you are responsible for:

- Executing baking procedures accurately and safely.
- Managing ingredients and logging production in PantryLedger.
- Operating the enchanted oven under Shift Lead supervision.
- Identifying and responding to overactive pastry events.
- Maintaining back-of-house cleanliness and charm safety.

---

## Section 1: The Enchanted Oven

The enchanted oven is the most important and most dangerous piece of equipment in the bakery. Every Apprentice Baker must:

1. Know the oven mode for every product. See [`enchanted-oven-temperature-guide.md`](../../01-business-units/bakery-operations/job-aids/enchanted-oven-temperature-guide.md).
2. Never ignite the oven without a Shift Lead present.
3. Know what each indicator color means.
4. Always use Cool Down mode before shutdown.

> **Golden rule:** If the indicator is blue and flashing, do not use the oven. Tell the Shift Lead.

## Section 3: PantryLedger for Bakers

You will use PantryLedger for:

- **Batch logging:** Record every batch you produce. Required by the magical food safety policy.
- **Stock checks:** Run the Daily Stock Check at opening.
- **Substitution logging:** If you use an approved substitute, log it immediately.
- **Restricted ingredient log:** The Shift Lead enters their authorization PIN; you enter the ingredient and quantity.

If PantryLedger is down, record all information on paper and enter it as soon as the system is available. Notify the Shift Lead.

---

## Section 4: Charm Residue and Surface Safety

- Apply charm-clearing solution between every batch that involves a different enchantment type.
- Never use silver-lined tools. They cause charm discharge.
- Log charm-residue clearing events in the batch log.
- If you notice charm residue that seems unusually resistant to clearing, notify the Shift Lead before continuing production.

---

## Section 5: Overactive Pastry Response

You are likely to be the first person to notice an overactive pastry event during production. Your responsibility is:

1. Stop what you are doing.
2. Observe the item without touching it.
3. Assign a severity level using the [`pastry-behavior-severity-table.md`](../../01-business-units/bakery-operations/job-aids/pastry-behavior-severity-table.md).
4. Follow the steps in [`handle-overactive-pastry.md`](../../01-business-units/bakery-operations/procedures/handle-overactive-pastry.md).
5. **For Level 3 or 4: do not touch the item. Clear the area and call the Shift Lead immediately.**

---

## Section 6: Restricted Ingredient Rules

- You may **never** access the restricted ingredient cabinet alone.
- You must verbally request authorization from the Shift Lead before retrieving any restricted ingredient.
- The Shift Lead must be physically present during retrieval.
- Unused restricted ingredient must be returned to the cabinet immediately after the batch.

Full policy: [`restricted-ingredient-policy.md`](../../01-business-units/bakery-operations/policies/restricted-ingredient-policy.md)

---

## Section 7: Closing and Cleaning

Your closing responsibilities in the back-of-house:

- Clear all charm residue from all surfaces and bowls.
- Complete your section of the [`cleaning-and-reset-checklist.md`](../../01-business-units/bakery-operations/job-aids/cleaning-and-reset-checklist.md).
- Return all ingredient bins to shelves with lids secured.
- Confirm the restricted ingredient cabinet is locked.
- Log the End-of-Day Inventory Snapshot in PantryLedger.
