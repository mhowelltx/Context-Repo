# Bakery Operations Knowledge Check

Complete this assessment at the end of your onboarding period. Review your answers with your Shift Lead.

**Total questions:** 20 · **Passing score:** 16/20 (80%)

---

## Part 1: Opening and Closing (Questions 1–5)

**Q1.** What is the minimum time before opening that you should arrive for an opening shift?

**Q2.** The enchanted oven's status indicator is flashing blue at startup. What do you do?
- A) Proceed with ignition — the blue light is the warm-up color
- B) Do not ignite; notify the Shift Lead
- C) Restart the runestone panel twice
- D) Call IT support directly

**Q3.** The opening float does not match the expected amount. What is the correct action?
- A) Add money from your wallet to make it balance
- B) Open the register and note the discrepancy later
- C) Do not open the register; notify the Shift Lead
- D) Close and recount three times before escalating

**Q4.** True or False: You may leave enchanted goods in the display case overnight if they are properly labeled.

**Q5.** What mode must the enchanted oven be set to before a full shutdown?

---

## Part 2: Production (Questions 6–10)

**Q6.** A sourdough starter has no bubbles and is silent. What do you do?
- A) Use it — the enchantment will activate during baking
- B) Feed it and wait 30 minutes
- C) Flag it in PantryLedger and notify the Shift Lead
- D) Substitute with the emergency dry starter without approval

**Q7.** What is the correct oven mode for dragon-pepper rolls?
- A) Standard Bake at 375°E
- B) Enchanted Mode at 300°E
- C) Thermal Bake at 420°E
- D) Standard Bake at 420°E

**Q8.** When should you load the melody stone into a singing cake?
- A) Before the crumb coat
- B) Before the final frosting layer
- C) After all decoration is complete
- D) During the baking step

**Q9.** True or False: You may use sea salt as a substitute for moon-salt when the supply is low.

**Q10.** Which of the following is NOT required in a PantryLedger batch log entry?
- A) Product name
- B) Baker name
- C) Customer's name
- D) Quantity produced

---

## Part 3: Overactive Pastry (Questions 11–13)

**Q11.** A dragon-pepper roll on the cooling rack is emitting sparks. What severity level is this?
- A) Level 1
- B) Level 2
- C) Level 3
- D) Level 4

**Q12.** At what level do you clear the area and notify the Shift Lead immediately, without touching the item?

**Q13.** True or False: A quarantined item may be returned to the display case if it appears normal after one hour, at your own discretion.

---

## Part 4: Customer Service (Questions 14–17)

**Q14.** A customer asks whether a dragon-pepper roll is safe for their fire-sensitive partner. What do you say?
- A) "Yes, the heat effect is very mild."
- B) "I can share what's in it, but I can't make a personal safety guarantee. Would you like to speak with our Shift Lead?"
- C) "No, it's definitely not safe for anyone with sensitivities."
- D) "Let me check online."

**Q15.** A customer wants a refund of 75 gold coins for a custom singing cake. Who must authorize this?

**Q16.** Which system do you use to look up a customer's custom order at pickup?
- A) CrumbKeeper POS
- B) PantryLedger
- C) Order Oracle
- D) The production board

**Q17.** True or False: You should improvise a response script when a customer reports harm from an overactive pastry.

---

## Part 5: Policies and Escalation (Questions 18–20)

**Q18.** According to the source-of-truth hierarchy, which takes precedence when there is a conflict?
- A) FAQ over procedure
- B) Business-unit policy over enterprise policy
- C) Enterprise policy over business-unit policy
- D) Training scenario over procedure

**Q19.** A customer reports a claim they believe may involve legal liability. What do you do?
- A) Explain the bakery's legal position
- B) Offer a refund to avoid conflict
- C) Do not engage further; notify the Shift Lead immediately
- D) Document in Order Oracle and handle it yourself

**Q20.** True or False: You may access the restricted ingredient cabinet alone if the Shift Lead is busy and production is urgent.
