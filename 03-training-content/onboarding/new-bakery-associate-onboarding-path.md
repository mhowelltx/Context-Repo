# New Bakery Associate Onboarding Path

Welcome to Grumble & Crumb Goblin Bakery. This onboarding path guides you through your first two weeks. Complete each step in order. Your Shift Lead will confirm completion and sign off at each stage.

---

## Before Your First Day

- [ ] Receive your associate keycard from the bakery manager.
- [ ] Set up your system logins: CrumbKeeper, PantryLedger, Order Oracle. (IT support: ext. 42)
- [ ] Review the [`00-start-here/overview.md`](../../00-start-here/overview.md) and [`how-to-use-this-repo.md`](../../00-start-here/how-to-use-this-repo.md).

## Week 2: Applied Practice

### Days 6–8 — Supervised Practice

- Complete all scenario examples in [`03-training-content/scenario-based-examples/`](../scenario-based-examples/).
- Shadow experienced associates during customer interactions.
- Practice at least two full morning openings under supervision.
- Log at least one batch in PantryLedger independently.

### Day 9 — Overactive Pastry and Escalation

| Task | Resource |
|---|---|
| Read overactive pastry procedure | [`procedures/handle-overactive-pastry.md`](../../01-business-units/bakery-operations/procedures/handle-overactive-pastry.md) |
| Review severity table | [`job-aids/pastry-behavior-severity-table.md`](../../01-business-units/bakery-operations/job-aids/pastry-behavior-severity-table.md) |
| Review escalation policy | [`policies/customer-incident-escalation-policy.md`](../../01-business-units/bakery-operations/policies/customer-incident-escalation-policy.md) |
| Review escalation scenario | [`scenario-based-examples/scenario-runaway-moon-glaze.md`](../scenario-based-examples/scenario-runaway-moon-glaze.md) |

### Day 10 — Knowledge Check and Sign-Off

- [ ] Complete the knowledge check: [`bakery-operations-knowledge-check.md`](../assessments/bakery-operations-knowledge-check.md)
- [ ] Review answers with Shift Lead.
- [ ] Shift Lead completes onboarding sign-off.
