# Scenario: Dragon-Pepper Allergy Question

> **Training scenario.** Illustrative example only. Follow the linked procedures for official guidance.

---

## Scenario Overview

**Setting:** Wednesday morning. A customer, Torwald Fenn, approaches the counter and points to the dragon-pepper rolls in the display case.

**Question:** *"Are those safe? My husband has a fire sensitivity."*

---

## What Happens

**Step 1: Do Not Guess or Guarantee**

Associate Midge does not immediately say yes or no. She knows she cannot make a personal safety guarantee. She takes a breath and responds:

> *"That's an important question — let me make sure I give you the right information."*

**Step 2: Describe the Ingredients**

Midge reads from the product label (does not recite from memory):

> *"Dragon-pepper rolls contain dragon-pepper extract, which carries a thermal enchantment. They also contain glimmer-wheat flour and goblin butter. The thermal enchantment is mild but present — the rolls stay warm longer than a standard roll and have a heating effect when eaten."*

**Step 3: Set an Honest Boundary**

Midge uses language from the [`allergen-and-charm-disclosure-policy.md`](../../01-business-units/bakery-operations/policies/allergen-and-charm-disclosure-policy.md):

> *"I can tell you exactly what's in them, but I'm not able to make a safety guarantee for individual sensitivities. Everyone responds differently to thermal enchantments."*

**Step 4: Offer to Escalate**

Midge uses the dragon-pepper script from [`customer-apology-scripts.md`](../../01-business-units/bakery-operations/job-aids/customer-apology-scripts.md) (Scenario 7):

> *"I'd suggest speaking with our Shift Lead if you have a specific sensitivity — they can provide more detail. Would you like me to get them for you?"*

Torwald says: *"Yes, please."*

**Step 5: Shift Lead Handles**

Shift Lead Bramblestitch comes to the counter. She confirms the ingredient list and the thermal enchantment description. She adds:

> *"If your husband has a documented fire sensitivity, we'd recommend avoiding the dragon-pepper rolls to be safe. Our mushroom hand pies are available charm-stable — no enchantment at all — if that's a better fit."*

Torwald thanks them and purchases two mushroom hand pies instead.

---

## What Went Well

- Midge did not guess or say the rolls were "safe."
- She read from the label, not from memory.
- She used the correct script language.
- She offered the Shift Lead when the question went beyond her scope.
- The Shift Lead offered a suitable alternative product.

## What to Avoid

- **Do not:** Say *"I think it should be fine"* or *"Most people are okay with it."*
- **Do not:** Make a judgment about the customer's husband's condition.
- **Do not:** Sell the rolls without disclosure if a sensitivity concern has been raised.

## Key Policy Reference

[`allergen-and-charm-disclosure-policy.md`](../../01-business-units/bakery-operations/policies/allergen-and-charm-disclosure-policy.md) — Section: *Responding to Sensitivity Questions*

> *"Describe what the product contains based on the label. State clearly: 'I can tell you what's in it, but I can't make a safety guarantee for individual sensitivities.' Offer to get the Shift Lead if the customer needs further guidance. Do not say a product is 'safe' for any individual customer."*
