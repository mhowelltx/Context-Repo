# Scenario: Clean Morning Open

> **Training scenario.** This is an illustrative example, not an authoritative procedure. Follow the linked procedures for official guidance.

---

## Scenario Overview

**Setting:** Tuesday morning, 7:00 AM. You are the opening associate. Shift Lead Bramblestitch is present. The bakery opens at 8:00 AM.

**Goal:** Complete a clean morning open with no issues.

---

## What Happens

**7:00 AM — Entry and Safety Check**

You unlock the bakery. You walk through the back-of-house. Everything looks normal. The display case is empty. The cooling rack quarantine area is clear. The enchantment containment bins are all sealed. You initial that section of the checklist.

**7:05 AM — System Startup**

You power on the CrumbKeeper terminal and log in. Your name appears in the top right. You open PantryLedger on the back-of-house tablet and check Order Oracle. There are two custom orders in the queue: a singing cake scheduled for 3:00 PM and a dozen mushroom hand pies for noon pickup. Neither is flagged overdue.

You notify Shift Lead Bramblestitch: *"Two orders today: singing cake at 3 PM and hand pies at noon. Nothing overdue."* She nods and notes the singing cake on the production board.

**7:10 AM — Ingredient Check**

You run the Daily Stock Check in PantryLedger. One item shows below par: filtered moonwater. You check the [`ingredient-substitution-table.md`](../../01-business-units/bakery-operations/job-aids/ingredient-substitution-table.md) — the approved substitute is filtered well-water from the on-site well, at a 1:1 ratio. No Shift Lead approval is needed for this substitution. You note the substitution and log it in the batch log before production starts.

**7:15 AM — Oven Startup**

Shift Lead Bramblestitch joins you at the oven. You inspect the exterior — no cracks or residue. You open the runestone panel, insert today's runestone token, and hold the green ignition rune. The oven hums and the indicator glows amber. You start the 20-minute warm-up timer.

**7:30 AM — Production Prep**

The oven reaches temperature (indicator turns green at 7:35 AM). You retrieve the morning's ingredients. You check the sourdough starter — it's bubbling well and murmuring quietly. A healthy starter.

You begin the mushroom hand pie filling prep since those are due first.

**7:45 AM — Display Case and Counter**

You load the overnight items from the production shelf into the display case. You apply today's date and charm-disclosure labels. The front counter is stocked with tongs, receipt rolls, and apology script cards.

**7:50 AM — Register Opening**

You open the register in CrumbKeeper: **Register > Open Shift**. You count the float: 40 gold coins. Expected: 40 gold coins. Perfect match. You confirm the open.

**7:55 AM — Final Check**

You complete the morning opening checklist. All items checked. Shift Lead Bramblestitch signs off. You flip the sign to **OPEN** at exactly 8:00 AM.

---

## What Went Well

- Ingredient shortfall was identified early and a documented substitute was applied.
- Systems were checked in the correct order.
- Shift Lead was present for oven ignition.
- Float matched exactly before opening.

## Key Decisions Made

| Situation | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Moonwater below par | Used approved well-water substitute | Listed in substitution table; no Shift Lead approval needed |
| Two custom orders in queue | Notified Shift Lead; prioritized hand pies | Hand pies due earlier; singing cake needs melody preparation |
| Register float correct | Opened register normally | Float matched — no escalation needed |
