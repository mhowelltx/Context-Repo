# Scenario: Runaway Moon-Glaze Incident

> **Training scenario.** Illustrative example only. Follow the linked procedures for official guidance.

---

## Scenario Overview

**Setting:** Thursday evening, 30 minutes before closing. Apprentice Baker Grix has just pulled a tray of moon-glaze buns from the Standard Bake oven. While placing them on the cooling rack, one bun begins pulsing with intense light — far brighter than the normal soft glow. The light begins spreading to the adjacent buns.

---

## What Happens

**Step 1: Recognize the Behavior**

Grix knows moon-glaze buns should have a soft, steady glow. This bun is pulsing brightly and appears to be spreading luminescence to the others. He refers mentally to the [`pastry-behavior-severity-table.md`](../../01-business-units/bakery-operations/job-aids/pastry-behavior-severity-table.md).

- Pulsing light: **Level 2**.
- Glow spreading to surfaces and adjacent items: **escalating toward Level 3**.

> Decision: When in doubt, assign the higher level. Grix calls this a **Level 2 escalating to Level 3** and acts accordingly.

**Step 2: Do Not Touch**

Grix does not reach for the bun. Level 3 behavior means do not touch. He steps back from the cooling rack.

**Step 3: Clear the Area**

Grix calls out calmly to Front Counter Associate Pidge: *"I need the back cleared for a moment."* Pidge moves to the front counter. No customers are in the back-of-house.

**Step 4: Notify Shift Lead Immediately**

Grix calls out: *"Bramblestitch, I need you — Level 3 on the cooling rack, moon-glaze buns."*

Shift Lead Bramblestitch arrives. She observes the buns from a distance. The pulsing has intensified but not produced heat or sparks. She determines this is a **Level 2–3 luminescent event** — no immediate evacuation needed, but no one touches the rack.

**Step 5: Wait and Monitor**

Bramblestitch instructs Grix to observe from a safe distance. After 8 minutes, the pulsing slows. After 12 minutes, it stops. The buns return to a normal soft glow.

**Step 6: Assessment and Disposition**

Bramblestitch examines the buns at arm's length with a light probe. She determines the enchantment has stabilized at normal level. She clears the batch for disposal (not sale — post-incident items are not sold without full assessment). She notes this in the batch log.

**Step 7: Log in Order Oracle**

Grix opens Order Oracle: **Incidents > New Incident**.
- Item: Moon-Glaze Buns, Batch #GL-0501-07
- Behavior: Intense pulsing luminescence spreading to adjacent items
- Severity: Level 2–3
- Action: Area cleared; Shift Lead notified; monitored 12 minutes until resolved; batch disposed
- Associates: Grix, Bramblestitch

Ticket submitted. Grix notes the ticket number on the quarantine tag he placed on the disposed buns.

---

## What Went Well

- Grix identified the behavior correctly and did not touch a potentially Level 3 item.
- He cleared the area without alarming customers.
- He notified the Shift Lead immediately and clearly.
- The Shift Lead made the disposition decision — not the Apprentice Baker.
- The incident was logged completely in Order Oracle.

## What to Avoid

- **Do not:** Attempt to move the item with your hands during active escalating behavior.
- **Do not:** Return to production without Shift Lead clearance.
- **Do not:** Sell buns from a batch that exhibited Level 2+ behavior without Shift Lead assessment.
- **Do not:** Skip the Order Oracle log because "it resolved itself."

## What Caused This?

Post-incident review determined that moon-salt was accidentally used instead of sea salt in this batch (a restricted ingredient). Moon-salt amplifies luminescent enchantments. This is why it is on the restricted ingredient list. See [`restricted-ingredient-policy.md`](../../01-business-units/bakery-operations/policies/restricted-ingredient-policy.md).
