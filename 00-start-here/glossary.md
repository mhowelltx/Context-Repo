# Bakery and Operations Glossary

This glossary defines terms used across the Grumble & Crumb Operations Manual. When a term appears in a procedure or policy, this is the authoritative definition.

---

## A

**Allergen Disclosure**
The required communication to a customer about the presence of common allergens (e.g., moon-nuts, glimmer-wheat, toadstool spores) in a product. See [`allergen-and-charm-disclosure-policy.md`](../01-business-units/bakery-operations/policies/allergen-and-charm-disclosure-policy.md).

**Apprentice Baker**
A bakery associate in training assigned to back-of-house production tasks. Responsible for mixing, baking, and decorating under Shift Lead supervision.

---

## B

**Batch Log**
The production record entered in PantryLedger documenting each baking batch: items produced, ingredient lot numbers, oven settings, and the baker's name.

**Bakery Operations Enablement**
The internal team responsible for maintaining procedures, policies, training content, and this knowledge base.

---

## C

**Charm Disclosure**
The required communication to a customer about the enchantment type present in a product (e.g., auditory, luminescent, thermal). Required before sale of any enchanted item. See [`allergen-and-charm-disclosure-policy.md`](../01-business-units/bakery-operations/policies/allergen-and-charm-disclosure-policy.md).

**Charm Residue**
Residual enchantment energy left in mixing bowls, baking pans, or surfaces after production of an enchanted item. Must be cleared before switching product types. See [`magical-food-safety-policy.md`](../01-business-units/bakery-operations/policies/magical-food-safety-policy.md).

**Charm-Stable**
A product or ingredient that has been verified to have no active enchantment effect. Mushroom Hand Pies are produced charm-stable by default.

**Closing Reset**
The end-of-shift procedure for cleaning, securing equipment, logging out of systems, and resetting the bakery for the next morning. See [`close-evening-shift.md`](../01-business-units/bakery-operations/procedures/close-evening-shift.md).

**Cooling Rack Quarantine**
The procedure of isolating a baked item on a designated rack when it exhibits unexpected or unsafe behavior. The item is tagged, logged, and assessed before any further handling or sale. See [`handle-overactive-pastry.md`](../01-business-units/bakery-operations/procedures/handle-overactive-pastry.md).

**CrumbKeeper POS**
The point-of-sale system used at the front counter for sales, refunds, and order lookup. See [`crumbkeeper-pos-guide.md`](../01-business-units/bakery-operations/systems/crumbkeeper-pos-guide.md).

**Crumb Surge**
An uncontrolled release of baking energy from an overactive pastry, typically resulting in scattered crumbs, minor sparks, or unexpected aromas. Classified as a Level 2 pastry behavior event.

**Customer Incident**
Any event involving a customer complaint, injury, product malfunction, or escalation request. Must be logged in Order Oracle. See [`customer-incident-escalation-policy.md`](../01-business-units/bakery-operations/policies/customer-incident-escalation-policy.md).

---

## D

**Display Case Rotation**
The process of refreshing the front display case with freshly baked goods and removing items past their display window. Performed during opening and mid-shift.

**Dragon-Pepper Roll**
A spicy, smoke-tinged enchanted roll. Contains dragon-pepper extract, which requires charm disclosure. Not recommended for customers with fire sensitivities or tinder-based materials.

---

## E

**Enchanted Oven**
A dual-mode oven capable of standard and magical baking temperatures. Features a runestone ignition panel and color-coded status indicators. See [`enchanted-oven-temperature-guide.md`](../01-business-units/bakery-operations/job-aids/enchanted-oven-temperature-guide.md).

**Escalation**
The process of routing a situation to a higher authority (Shift Lead, Manager, or external contact) when it exceeds the associate's authority or capability. See [`customer-incident-escalation-policy.md`](../01-business-units/bakery-operations/policies/customer-incident-escalation-policy.md).

---

## F

**Front Counter Associate**
An associate assigned to customer-facing duties: taking orders, handling sales, processing refunds, and managing customer interactions.

---

## G

**Goblin-Safe Handling**
A set of handling practices adapted for goblin physiology, including resistance to minor heat, sensitivity to silver-dusted ingredients, and preference for low-light workspaces.

---

## I

**Ingredient Substitution**
Replacing a standard ingredient with an approved alternative when the original is unavailable. See [`ingredient-substitution-table.md`](../01-business-units/bakery-operations/job-aids/ingredient-substitution-table.md).

**Invisible Shortbread**
A shortbread product with a light-refracting enchantment that renders it visually transparent. Standard in all other respects.

---

## M

**Moon-Glaze**
A luminescent glaze applied to buns or rolls at midnight under controlled light conditions. Provides a soft glow effect and mild sweetness boost.

**Moon-Glaze Bun**
A glazed bun produced with moon-glaze. Requires overnight production scheduling. Glaze loses luminescence after 12 hours at room temperature.

**Mushroom Hand Pie**
A savory hand pie with an earthy mushroom filling. Available unenchanted by customer request.

---

## O

**Order Oracle**
The order management and incident ticketing system. Used for custom cake orders, order status tracking, and customer incident logging. See [`order-oracle-ticketing-guide.md`](../01-business-units/bakery-operations/systems/order-oracle-ticketing-guide.md).

**Order Status**
The current state of a customer order in Order Oracle (e.g., Pending, In Production, Ready for Pickup, Fulfilled, Cancelled). See [`04-reference/status-definitions/order-status-definitions.md`](../04-reference/status-definitions/order-status-definitions.md).

**Overactive Pastry**
A baked item displaying unintended or amplified enchantment behavior beyond its normal parameters. May include unexpected singing, levitation, heat emission, or rapid expansion. Requires immediate isolation. See [`handle-overactive-pastry.md`](../01-business-units/bakery-operations/procedures/handle-overactive-pastry.md).

---

## P

**PantryLedger**
The inventory management system used to track ingredient stock, log restocking, and generate low-stock alerts. See [`pantry-inventory-system-guide.md`](../01-business-units/bakery-operations/systems/pantry-inventory-system-guide.md).

**Pastry Behavior Level**
A 1–4 severity classification for overactive pastry incidents. Level 1 = minor anomaly; Level 4 = immediate evacuation required. See [`pastry-behavior-severity-table.md`](../01-business-units/bakery-operations/job-aids/pastry-behavior-severity-table.md).

---

## R

**Refund Reason Code**
A standardized code entered in CrumbKeeper when processing a refund. Codes identify the reason category (e.g., RRC-01 Incorrect Order, RRC-04 Enchantment Failure). See [`04-reference/code-lists/refund-reason-codes.md`](../04-reference/code-lists/refund-reason-codes.md).

**Remake**
Producing a replacement product for a customer at no charge due to a product defect or order error. Distinct from a refund. See [`refund-and-remake-policy.md`](../01-business-units/bakery-operations/policies/refund-and-remake-policy.md).

**Restricted Ingredient**
An ingredient that requires Shift Lead authorization before use, due to potency, scarcity, or potential for charm amplification. See [`restricted-ingredient-policy.md`](../01-business-units/bakery-operations/policies/restricted-ingredient-policy.md).

---

## S

**Shift Lead**
The senior associate responsible for shift operations, escalations, and supervisory decisions during a given shift.

**Singing Cake**
A custom celebration cake that plays a short pre-loaded melody when the candles are lit. The song is selected at the time of order. Melody issues are the most common singing cake complaint type.

**Sourdough Starter (Whispering)**
The live culture used to leaven whispering sourdough. Requires daily feeding and must be stored in the designated enchantment-stable container.

---

## W

**Whispering Sourdough**
A long-fermented sourdough loaf with a low-level auditory enchantment. The loaf murmurs bread-related advice when handled. Volume fades within 24 hours of baking.
