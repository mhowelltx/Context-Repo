## [0.1.0] — 2026-05-01

### Added

- Initial repository structure with all top-level folders and README.
- `00-start-here/`: Overview, how-to-use guide, agent instructions, and full glossary (32 terms).
- `01-business-units/bakery-operations/`: Eight procedures, five policies, six job aids, four system guides, and one FAQ with 20 questions.
- `01-business-units/bakery-operations/process-map.md`: Mermaid flowchart of end-to-end bakery operations.
- `02-shared-knowledge/`: Guest service principles, magical workplace safety basics, enterprise escalation principles, and bakery terminology reference.
- `03-training-content/`: New associate onboarding path, front counter training guide, apprentice baker training guide, four scenario examples, and a knowledge check with answer key.
- `04-reference/`: Custom cake order form fields, refund reason codes, order status definitions, customer complaint routing table, pastry behavior response table, and CrumbKeeper field definitions.
- `05-archive/`: Two sample archived documents (deprecated singing cake remake process, superseded oven safety checklist).
- `90-templates/`: Five reusable templates (procedure, policy, FAQ, job aid, training scenario).
- `99-admin/`: Content inventory CSV, document owners list, review calendar, and source-of-truth map.
- `CONTRIBUTING.md`: Full contribution guide including naming conventions, front matter standards, and archiving instructions.
- `GOVERNANCE.md`: Full governance document including ownership, review cycles, approval rules, conflict resolution, and AI agent usage expectations.

### Notes

- This is the initial demo release. All content is fictional.
- No real business processes, customer data, or regulatory claims are included.
- AI agent integration is configured via `00-start-here/agent-instructions.md`.
