# Prepare Dragon-Pepper Rolls

## Purpose

Produce dragon-pepper rolls safely, ensuring all thermal enchantment and allergen handling requirements are met.

## Audience

Apprentice Bakers. Shift Lead authorization required before handling dragon-pepper extract.

## Prerequisites

- Shift Lead has authorized dragon-pepper extract retrieval from the secured cabinet.
- Heat-resistant gloves are in place at the prep station.
- Enchanted oven set to **Thermal Bake mode: 420°E**.
- Allergen disclosure cards stocked at the front counter.

---

## Steps

### 1. Ingredient Retrieval

1. Obtain Shift Lead signature in PantryLedger under **Restricted Ingredients Log** before retrieving dragon-pepper extract.
2. Retrieve extract from the secured spice cabinet using the provided key.
3. Return the key to the Shift Lead immediately after retrieval.
4. Do not leave dragon-pepper extract unattended on the prep surface.

> **Warning:** Dragon-pepper extract can cause thermal burns if it contacts bare skin. Always wear heat-resistant gloves during handling.

### 2. Dough Preparation

1. Weigh ingredients:
   - All-purpose charm flour: 450g
   - Active dry yeast: 7g
   - Warm moonwater: 280g
   - Sea salt: 8g
   - Rendered goblin butter: 40g
   - Dragon-pepper extract: 15ml (exactly — do not estimate)
2. Combine moonwater and yeast. Allow to bloom 5 minutes.
3. Add flour, salt, and butter. Mix until a smooth dough forms.
4. Add dragon-pepper extract last. Mix thoroughly until evenly distributed.
5. The dough will feel slightly warm to the touch through gloves. This is normal.

### 3. First Rise

1. Cover dough with the heat-resistant proofing dome.
2. Allow to rise in a warm spot (75–80°F) for 60–90 minutes until doubled.
3. If the dough begins to smoke or emit sparks during proofing, do not touch it. Notify the Shift Lead and initiate cooling rack quarantine.

### 4. Shape the Rolls

1. Punch down the dough. Divide into 12 equal portions (approximately 68g each).
2. Roll each portion into a smooth ball. The surface may have a slight reddish tinge — this is expected.
3. Place rolls in the greased Thermal Baking Pan, evenly spaced.
4. Cover with the heat-resistant dome. Allow to proof a second time: 30 minutes.

### 5. Bake

1. Set the enchanted oven to **Thermal Bake mode: 420°E**.
2. Open oven with heat-resistant gloves. The interior will be significantly hotter than Standard Bake mode.
3. Place the Thermal Baking Pan on the center rack.
4. Bake 18–22 minutes until rolls are deep red-brown and emit a thin wisp of smoke.
5. The smoke is normal and will dissipate in 60 seconds.
6. Remove pan with gloves. Do not touch the rolls bare-handed for 10 minutes post-bake.

### 6. Quality Check and Labeling

1. Rolls should be deep red-brown, firm, and have a mild smoky aroma.
2. Each roll must be labeled: **Dragon-Pepper Roll — THERMAL ENCHANTMENT — Contains Dragon-Pepper Extract.**
3. Place allergen disclosure cards in the display case alongside the rolls.
4. Log batch in PantryLedger.

---

## Exceptions

- If dragon-pepper extract quantity is below minimum (15ml), do not substitute with chili powder or any other spice. The roll cannot be made that day. Notify Shift Lead.
- Do not use Standard Bake mode for dragon-pepper rolls. Under-baking can cause unstable thermal enchantment.

## Common Mistakes

- Forgetting heat-resistant gloves during extract handling.
- Under-measuring dragon-pepper extract and producing bland (non-enchanted) rolls.
- Placing rolls in a non-Thermal pan, which can warp at bake temperature.
- Skipping the allergen disclosure card at the display case.

## Escalation Path

- Dough smoking or sparking during proofing: Shift Lead immediately.
- Burns or injury during handling: Shift Lead, then first aid kit (under front counter).
- Unauthorized access to dragon-pepper extract: Shift Lead and document in Order Oracle.
