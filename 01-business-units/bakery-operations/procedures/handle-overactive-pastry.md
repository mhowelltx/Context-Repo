# Handle Overactive Pastry

## Purpose

Respond safely to a baked item displaying unintended or amplified enchantment behavior. Protect associates and customers from harm. Log the incident correctly.

## Audience

All bakery associates. Every associate must know this procedure. Shift Lead is the decision-maker for Level 3 and Level 4 events.

## Prerequisites

- Cooling rack quarantine area is cleared and accessible (rear of back-of-house).
- Quarantine tags and a pen are available.
- Order Oracle is accessible.

---

## Steps

### 1. Identify and Assess

1. Observe the pastry's behavior without touching it.
2. Identify the behavior type (auditory, thermal, kinetic, aromatic, visual).
3. Assign a severity level using the [`pastry-behavior-severity-table.md`](../job-aids/pastry-behavior-severity-table.md):

| Level | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Minor anomaly, contained | Sourdough murmuring louder than usual |
| 2 | Noticeable but not hazardous | Moon-glaze bun pulsing light, crumb surge |
| 3 | Potentially hazardous | Dragon-pepper roll emitting sparks, cake singing on loop |
| 4 | Immediate risk | Open flame from roll, levitating heavy item, structural damage |

### 2. Isolate (All Levels)

1. Clear a path to the cooling rack quarantine area.
2. If Level 1 or 2: use heat-resistant tongs or a charm-safe paddle to move the item to the cooling rack.
3. If Level 3 or 4: **do not touch the item.** Clear the area of all associates and customers. Notify the Shift Lead immediately.
4. Place a quarantine tag on the rack with: item name, time, behavior observed, and your name.

### 3. Notify

- **Level 1–2:** Notify Shift Lead at next convenient moment (within 15 minutes).
- **Level 3–4:** Notify Shift Lead immediately. Shift Lead notifies the bakery manager.

### 4. Log in Order Oracle

1. Open Order Oracle.
2. Navigate to **Incidents > New Incident**.
3. Complete all required fields:
   - Item name and batch number.
   - Behavior observed.
   - Severity level.
   - Action taken.
   - Associates present.
4. Submit the incident ticket.
5. Attach the quarantine tag number to the ticket.

### 5. Disposition of the Item

| Outcome | Action |
|---|---|
| Behavior resolves within 1 hour (Level 1) | Shift Lead may clear for sale at their discretion |
| Behavior persists | Do not sell. Dispose of per the Shift Lead's instruction |
| Level 3–4 item | Dispose of after Shift Lead approval. Never sell |
| Customer's custom order | Issue remake or refund per [`refund-and-remake-policy.md`](../policies/refund-and-remake-policy.md) |

### 6. Area Reset

1. After the item is removed or disposed of, check the surrounding area for charm residue.
2. Clean with the standard charm-clearing solution (in the supply closet).
3. Log the cleaning in the batch log.

---

## Common Mistakes

- Attempting to touch or move a Level 3–4 item without protective equipment.
- Failing to log the incident in Order Oracle.
- Returning a quarantined item to the display case without Shift Lead clearance.
- Not clearing customers from the area before addressing a Level 3 event.

## Escalation Path

- Level 3 or 4: Shift Lead immediately.
- If Shift Lead is unavailable: bakery manager via the back-of-house phone.
- Injury to any person: Shift Lead, first aid kit (under front counter), and file an incident report in Order Oracle.
