# Prepare Whispering Sourdough

## Purpose

Produce a consistent, properly enchanted whispering sourdough loaf that meets Grumble & Crumb quality standards.

## Audience

Apprentice Bakers. Shift Lead must confirm starter health before first batch of the week.

## Prerequisites

- Active whispering sourdough starter (fed within the last 8 hours).
- Glimmer-wheat flour, sea salt, and filtered moonwater in stock (verified in PantryLedger).
- Enchanted oven at operating temperature (Standard Bake mode: 375°E).
- Charm-safe mixing bowl (silver-free, verified clean).

---

## Steps

### 1. Starter Assessment

1. Open the enchantment-stable starter container. The starter should be bubbly and emit a faint whisper (this is normal).
2. If the starter smells sour but shows no bubbles, it needs feeding before use. Feed and wait 4–6 hours.
3. If the starter is silent and has no bubbles, flag it in PantryLedger and notify the Shift Lead. Do not use.
4. Log the starter health check in the batch log.

### 2. Mix the Dough

1. Weigh ingredients:
   - Glimmer-wheat flour: 500g
   - Filtered moonwater (room temperature): 375g
   - Active starter: 100g
   - Sea salt: 10g
2. Combine flour and moonwater in the mixing bowl. Mix until no dry flour remains. Rest 30 minutes (autolyse).
3. Add starter and salt. Mix by hand using the folding method for 5 minutes.
4. The dough should feel slightly tacky. If it is extremely sticky, add flour in 10g increments up to 30g maximum.
5. Log batch start in PantryLedger under **Batch Log > New Entry**.

### 3. Bulk Fermentation

1. Cover the mixing bowl with the enchantment-dampening cloth.
2. Allow to ferment at room temperature (65–70°F) for 4–6 hours.
3. Perform stretch-and-fold every 45 minutes for the first 3 hours (4 sets total).
4. The dough is ready when it has increased 50–75% in volume and the surface shows bubbles.

> **Note:** During fermentation, you may hear the dough begin to whisper softly. This indicates the enchantment is activating. Increased whisper volume is normal. Shouting is not normal — see [`handle-overactive-pastry.md`](handle-overactive-pastry.md).

### 4. Shape and Final Proof

1. Lightly dust the work surface with glimmer-wheat flour.
2. Turn the dough out and pre-shape into a round. Rest 20 minutes uncovered.
3. Final shape: fold and tension the loaf, place seam-side up in a proofing basket lined with the enchantment-dampening cloth.
4. Cover and refrigerate for 12–18 hours (overnight cold proof).

### 5. Bake

1. Preheat the enchanted oven to **375°E (Standard Bake mode)** with a Dutch oven inside.
2. Remove the loaf from the refrigerator. Score the top with the standard arc pattern.
3. Place loaf in the hot Dutch oven. Bake covered for 20 minutes.
4. Remove lid. Bake uncovered for an additional 25 minutes until deep golden.
5. Internal temperature should read 205°F on the standard probe (not the enchantment probe).
6. Remove from oven. Place on the standard cooling rack.
7. The whisper should intensify briefly on cooling, then settle to a murmur within 30 minutes.

### 6. Quality Check and Logging

1. Verify loaf color (deep golden, no pale patches), crust (firm), and whisper level (murmur, not shout).
2. If whisper is louder than ambient conversation level after 60 minutes of cooling, quarantine the loaf. See [`handle-overactive-pastry.md`](handle-overactive-pastry.md).
3. Log the completed batch in PantryLedger: quantity, weight, baker name, oven batch number.
4. Label the loaf with bake date, batch number, and charm type: **Auditory — Low**.

## Exceptions

- If moonwater is unavailable, do not substitute tap water. Use the approved filtered well-water substitute. See [`ingredient-substitution-table.md`](../job-aids/ingredient-substitution-table.md).
- Do not bake sourdough in Enchanted Mode. Standard Bake mode only.

## Common Mistakes

- Using a starter that has not been fed within 8 hours.
- Skipping the autolyse step, which weakens gluten development.
- Using a silver-lined bowl, which disrupts the enchantment.
- Baking in Enchanted Mode, which over-amplifies the auditory charm.

## Escalation Path

- Silent, inactive starter: Shift Lead.
- Shouting or unusually loud loaf post-bake: quarantine, then Shift Lead.
- PantryLedger logging failure: IT support.
