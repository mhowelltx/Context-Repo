# Customer Incident Escalation Policy

## Purpose

Define escalation triggers and response actions for customer incidents, ensuring that all situations are handled at the correct authority level without unnecessary delay.

## Scope

All customer-facing incidents at Grumble & Crumb, including complaints, product defects, safety events, and disputes.

---

## Policy Statement

Every customer incident must be addressed promptly, logged in Order Oracle, and escalated to the appropriate level based on the severity and nature of the event. Associates must not delay escalation to avoid conflict or inconvenience.

---

## Escalation Levels

### Level 1 — Associate Handles

Associate resolves without Shift Lead involvement.

**Triggers:**
- Minor complaint about taste or texture.
- Incorrect order corrected with an available replacement.
- Refund request under 20 gold coins for an eligible item.
- Customer question about allergens or enchantments (answered per policy).

**Actions:**
- Apply the appropriate apology script from [`customer-apology-scripts.md`](../job-aids/customer-apology-scripts.md).
- Offer remake or refund per [`refund-and-remake-policy.md`](refund-and-remake-policy.md).
- Log in Order Oracle if a refund is issued.

### Level 2 — Shift Lead Involved

Associate alerts Shift Lead, who takes an active role in resolution.

**Triggers:**
- Refund request between 20–100 gold coins.
- Customer is dissatisfied after associate's first resolution attempt.
- Product defect involving enchantment failure.
- Overactive pastry at Level 2 severity.
- Custom order dispute at pickup.

**Actions:**
- Associate notifies Shift Lead immediately.
- Shift Lead speaks directly with the customer.
- Shift Lead approves refund or remake as appropriate.
- Log in Order Oracle with Shift Lead name and resolution.

### Level 3 — Shift Lead + Bakery Manager

Shift Lead escalates to Bakery Manager.

**Triggers:**
- Refund request over 100 gold coins.
- Overactive pastry at Level 3 or Level 4 severity.
- Customer reports physical harm or injury.
- Claim of severe allergic reaction.
- Media presence or threat of public complaint.
- Repeated incidents involving the same product or associate.

**Actions:**
- Shift Lead contacts Bakery Manager immediately by phone.
- Do not discuss liability or make financial commitments beyond existing policy.
- Log full details in Order Oracle, including time, people present, and all actions taken.
- Preserve the implicated product for review if safely possible.

---

## All Incident Logging Requirements

Every incident at Level 2 or above must be logged in Order Oracle under **Incidents > New Incident**. Required fields:
- Customer name (if provided)
- Date and time
- Product involved
- Description of complaint or incident
- Resolution offered and outcome
- Associates present
- Shift Lead name

---

## Exception Handling

- If a Shift Lead is not available and a Level 2+ situation arises, the senior associate on shift handles it, notes the absence in Order Oracle, and contacts the Shift Lead as soon as possible.
