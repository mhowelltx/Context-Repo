# Magical Food Safety Policy

## Purpose

Establish minimum safety standards for the handling, production, storage, and sale of enchanted baked goods at Grumble & Crumb Goblin Bakery.

## Scope

This policy applies to all bakery associates, including Apprentice Bakers, Front Counter Associates, and Shift Leads, during all production and service activities.

---

## Policy Statement

All enchanted baked goods produced or sold at Grumble & Crumb must meet both standard food safety requirements and the additional handling requirements specific to their enchantment type. No product may be sold that poses an uncontrolled risk to customers or associates.

---

## Requirements

### 1. Ingredient Handling

- All magical ingredients must be sourced from approved vendors and logged in PantryLedger at time of receipt.
- Ingredients with active enchantment properties (e.g., dragon-pepper extract, glimmer-wheat flour, moon-glaze) must be stored in designated enchantment-stable containers.
- Expired or damaged magical ingredients must be disposed of and logged in PantryLedger. Do not use expired magical ingredients in any production batch.
- Restricted ingredients require Shift Lead authorization before use. See [`restricted-ingredient-policy.md`](restricted-ingredient-policy.md).

### 2. Surface and Equipment Safety

- All mixing bowls and surfaces used for enchanted production must be verified charm-free before switching product types.
- Use the charm-clearing solution (available in the supply closet) between batches of different enchantment types.
- Silver-lined or silver-coated tools must not be used for any enchanted baking. Silver disrupts charm stability.
- Charm residue left on surfaces can cross-contaminate subsequent batches. Log all charm-residue cleaning events in the batch log.

### 3. Overactive Pastry Response

- Any baked item displaying unintended enchantment behavior must be quarantined immediately.
- Do not sell any quarantined item without Shift Lead clearance.
- Follow [`handle-overactive-pastry.md`](../procedures/handle-overactive-pastry.md) for all overactive pastry incidents.
- All overactive pastry incidents must be logged in Order Oracle.

### 4. Display and Storage

- Enchanted goods must not be stored in close proximity to items with conflicting enchantment types (e.g., auditory items near thermal items).
- Whispering sourdough and singing cakes must not be stored on the same shelf — charm interference may cause one to amplify the other.
- Do not leave any enchanted item in the display case overnight. Unmonitored enchantment activity may amplify in low-light conditions.
- Invisible shortbread must be stored in a labeled container. Unlabeled invisible shortbread creates a slip hazard.

### 5. Customer Sale Standards

- All enchanted products must be disclosed to the customer before purchase. See [`allergen-and-charm-disclosure-policy.md`](allergen-and-charm-disclosure-policy.md).
- No product may be sold if it has exhibited Level 3 or Level 4 pastry behavior.
- If a product's enchantment has expired or faded significantly, it may still be sold but must be clearly labeled as **Charm-Faded**.

---

## Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the Shift Lead and must be logged in Order Oracle before the exception is applied. No exception may be applied that knowingly puts a customer or associate at risk.

---

## Review and Approval

This policy is reviewed quarterly by the Bakery Operations Enablement team. Changes require Shift Lead review and Knowledge Manager approval before taking effect.
