# Allergen and Charm Disclosure Policy

## Purpose

Ensure all customers receive accurate information about allergens and enchantment types in Grumble & Crumb products before purchase, enabling informed decisions.

## Scope

All sales and customer interactions at the front counter and custom order handoff.

---

## Policy Statement

Every customer who asks about ingredients or enchantments must receive complete and accurate disclosure. Associates must never guess or estimate allergen or enchantment content. When in doubt, check the product label or consult the Shift Lead.

---

## Requirements

### 1. Standard Allergen Disclosure

The following are common allergens present in Grumble & Crumb products. Associates must be able to identify which products contain each:

| Allergen | Found In |
|---|---|
| Glimmer-wheat (gluten analog) | Whispering Sourdough, Dragon-Pepper Rolls, Singing Cakes |
| Moon-nuts | Moon-Glaze Buns (glaze contains trace moon-nut oil) |
| Toadstool spores | Mushroom Hand Pies |
| Dragon-pepper extract | Dragon-Pepper Rolls only |
| Goblin butter (dairy analog) | All products except Invisible Shortbread (goblin-butter-free) |

- If a customer asks about allergens, read from the product label. Do not recite from memory.
- If the customer has a known severe sensitivity, recommend they speak directly with the Shift Lead.
- Do not make guarantees about cross-contact. All products are produced in a shared kitchen.

### 2. Charm Disclosure Requirements

All enchanted products must be disclosed before sale. The disclosure must include:

1. The enchantment type (e.g., auditory, thermal, luminescent, optical).
2. The expected behavior (e.g., "this loaf will murmur softly for up to 24 hours").
3. Any relevant handling instruction (e.g., "do not store near open flames; dragon-pepper rolls retain thermal charge for 2 hours post-bake").

**Charm Disclosure Cards** are available at the front counter and must be placed in the bag with every enchanted product purchase.

### 3. Custom Order Charm Disclosure

- For singing cakes, confirm the melody and enchantment type with the customer at pickup.
- For any custom order with a charm the customer did not specifically request, disclose it and confirm acceptance before handoff.

### 4. Responding to Sensitivity Questions

If a customer asks whether a product is safe for a specific sensitivity:

1. Describe what the product contains based on the label.
2. State clearly: *"I can tell you what's in it, but I can't make a safety guarantee for individual sensitivities."*
3. Offer to get the Shift Lead if the customer needs further guidance.
4. Do not say a product is "safe" for any individual customer.

---

## Exception Handling

- If a product label is missing or unclear, do not sell the product until it is labeled correctly.
- If a customer claims an allergic reaction after purchase, log it in Order Oracle immediately and notify the Shift Lead.

---

## Review and Approval

This policy is reviewed quarterly. Any change to the allergen list requires Shift Lead and Knowledge Manager approval.
