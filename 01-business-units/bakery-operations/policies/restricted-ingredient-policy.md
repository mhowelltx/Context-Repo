# Restricted Ingredient Policy

## Purpose

Control access to high-potency or scarce magical ingredients that could cause harm, batch failures, or charm amplification if misused.

## Scope

All bakery production activities involving restricted ingredients listed in this policy.

---

## Policy Statement

Restricted ingredients may only be retrieved and used with active Shift Lead authorization. No associate may access the restricted ingredient cabinet without a Shift Lead present and a log entry in PantryLedger.

---

## Current Restricted Ingredient List

| Ingredient | Reason for Restriction | Max Daily Use |
|---|---|---|
| Dragon-pepper extract | Thermal hazard; can cause burns if mishandled | 60ml per batch |
| Starlight reduction | Amplifies all enchantment types if over-used | 5ml per batch |
| Midnight bloom powder | Causes rapid fermentation; can over-proof in minutes | 2g per batch |
| Bottled goblin laughter | Auditory amplifier; can cause charm surges | 10ml per batch |
| Moon-salt (crystallized) | Reacts with silver surfaces — creates charm discharge | 15g per batch |

---

## Authorization Requirements

1. Before any restricted ingredient is retrieved:
   - The Apprentice Baker must verbally request authorization from the Shift Lead.
   - The Shift Lead must log the authorization in PantryLedger under **Restricted Ingredients Log**: ingredient name, quantity authorized, baker name, date, and time.
2. The Shift Lead must be physically present while the ingredient is retrieved from the cabinet.
3. The cabinet must be relocked immediately after retrieval. The Shift Lead holds the key.
4. Unused restricted ingredient must be returned to the cabinet at the end of the batch.

---

## Exception Handling

- No exceptions to the authorization requirement are permitted.
- If a Shift Lead is unavailable, production of any recipe requiring a restricted ingredient must be paused until a Shift Lead is on-site.
- If a restricted ingredient is found outside the cabinet without authorization, log it in Order Oracle as an incident and notify the bakery manager.

---

## Review and Approval

The restricted ingredient list is reviewed quarterly. New ingredients may be added by the Shift Lead with Bakery Manager approval. Removal from the list requires Bakery Manager approval.
