# Refund and Remake Policy

## Purpose

Define when a customer is entitled to a refund or a remake, and establish the authority levels and time limits for each.

## Scope

All customer transactions at the front counter and custom order pickups.

---

## Policy Statement

Grumble & Crumb stands behind the quality of every item it sells. Customers who receive a defective, incorrect, or unsafe product are entitled to a fair resolution. Associates should default to offering a remake before a refund when the item can be reproduced on the same day.

---

## Refund Eligibility

| Scenario | Eligible for Refund? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Incorrect item received | Yes | Full refund or remake |
| Enchantment failure (e.g., wrong song, faded glaze) | Yes | Remake preferred if same-day |
| Overactive pastry that reached customer | Yes | Full refund; log incident in Order Oracle |
| Customer changed their mind | No | Store credit at Shift Lead discretion |
| Product consumed more than 50% | No | Shift Lead exception required |
| Custom order wrong spelling | Yes | Remake preferred if pickup not yet occurred |
| Customer delayed pickup > 24 hours | No | Product quality no longer guaranteed |

## Refund Time Limits

- **Standard items:** Refund must be requested on the day of purchase.
- **Custom orders:** Refund or remake must be requested within 48 hours of the scheduled pickup time.
- Exceptions beyond these limits require Shift Lead approval and must be logged in Order Oracle.

## Remake vs. Refund Decision

Offer a **remake** when:
- The item can be produced on the same day.
- The error was Grumble & Crumb's fault (wrong item, wrong decoration, melody failure).
- The customer is willing to wait.

Offer a **refund** when:
- The item cannot be remade the same day.
- The customer prefers a refund.
- The issue involved safety (overactive pastry that reached the customer).

## Authority Levels

| Refund Amount | Authority Required |
|---|---|
| Up to 20 gold coins | Front Counter Associate |
| 20–100 gold coins | Shift Lead approval |
| Over 100 gold coins | Shift Lead + Bakery Manager |

---

## Exception Handling

- Remakes beyond one per transaction require Shift Lead approval.
- Refunds for items not in the CrumbKeeper system (e.g., very old receipt) require Shift Lead approval.
- Do not issue a refund for a product the customer claims was damaged after leaving the bakery without evidence of pre-existing defect.

---

## Review and Approval

Reviewed quarterly. Authority level thresholds may only be changed with Bakery Manager approval.
