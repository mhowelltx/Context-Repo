# Cleaning and Reset Checklist

Complete this checklist at the end of every shift. All items must be checked before the Shift Lead signs off.

> Full closing procedure: [`close-evening-shift.md`](../procedures/close-evening-shift.md)

---

## Front Counter

- [ ] Display case wiped inside and outside
- [ ] All display items removed (no enchanted goods left overnight)
- [ ] Day-two items wrapped, labeled, and stored on day-two shelf
- [ ] Expired items disposed of and logged in PantryLedger
- [ ] Counter surface wiped down
- [ ] Pastry tongs washed and returned to holder
- [ ] Charm-disclosure card holder restocked
- [ ] Receipt roll replaced if low
- [ ] POS terminal logged out and powered off

## Back-of-House Production Area

- [ ] All mixing bowls washed and charm-residue-cleared
- [ ] Charm-clearing solution applied to all prep surfaces
- [ ] Ingredient bins sealed and returned to shelves
- [ ] Batch log entries completed in PantryLedger
- [ ] Dutch oven and Thermal Baking Pan washed and stored
- [ ] Proofing baskets brushed clean and stacked
- [ ] Sourdough starter container sealed and stored in designated location
- [ ] Melody stone cabinet locked
- [ ] Decoration station surfaces wiped; piping bags washed or disposed of

## Enchanted Oven

- [ ] Oven placed in Cool Down mode (not powered off directly)
- [ ] Cool Down complete — indicator is white
- [ ] Runestone token removed with tongs and returned to Shift Lead lockbox
- [ ] Runestone panel closed and latched
- [ ] Oven exterior wiped for charm residue

## Restricted Ingredient Cabinet

- [ ] All restricted ingredients returned to cabinet
- [ ] Cabinet locked — Shift Lead confirmed
- [ ] Any unused restricted ingredient quantity logged in PantryLedger

## Supply Room

- [ ] Supply room tidy; no spills or pest activity observed
- [ ] Supply room door locked
- [ ] Supply room key returned to Shift Lead

## Systems

- [ ] PantryLedger: End-of-Day Inventory Snapshot run and saved
- [ ] Order Oracle: All tickets closed or tagged for next day
- [ ] All systems logged out

## Final Security

- [ ] All windows closed and latched
- [ ] Back entrance locked
- [ ] Enchantment containment bins sealed
- [ ] Cooling rack quarantine area cleared (or: any tagged items documented)
- [ ] Shift Lead walkthrough complete
- [ ] Front door locked by Shift Lead
