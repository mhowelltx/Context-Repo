# Pastry Behavior Severity Table

Use this table to quickly assess and classify overactive pastry behavior. Match observed behavior to the level. Then follow the corresponding response.

> For full handling procedure: [`handle-overactive-pastry.md`](../procedures/handle-overactive-pastry.md)
> For response routing: [`pastry-behavior-response-table.md`](../../../04-reference/decision-tables/pastry-behavior-response-table.md)

---

## Severity Levels

| Level | Name | Observable Behavior | Immediate Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Minor Anomaly | Louder-than-normal whispering; slight glow beyond normal luminosity; mild unexpected aroma | Move to cooling rack with tongs; tag; notify Shift Lead within 15 min |
| **2** | Noticeable Event | Pulsing light; audible beyond adjacent counter; crumb surge (scattered crumbs); mild warmth beyond expected | Move to cooling rack; quarantine; log in Order Oracle; notify Shift Lead |
| **3** | Potentially Hazardous | Sparks; continuous unintended melody; elevated heat output; levitation of light items; smoke | Do not touch. Clear area. Notify Shift Lead immediately. |
| **4** | Immediate Risk | Open flame; sustained levitation of heavy items; structural cracking of pan or rack; continuous loud noise; chemical-like aroma | Do not touch. Evacuate area. Shift Lead calls Bakery Manager. Follow emergency protocol. |

---

## Product-Specific Behavior Notes

| Product | Normal Behavior | Watch For (Escalate) |
|---|---|---|
| Whispering Sourdough | Soft murmur; fades within 24 hrs | Volume louder than ambient conversation; hissing |
| Moon-Glaze Buns | Soft glow; fades after 12 hrs | Pulsing glow; heat emission; glow spreading to surfaces |
| Dragon-Pepper Rolls | Mild warmth; slight smoke on removal from oven | Persistent smoke; sparks; heat increasing over time |
| Singing Cake | Melody plays only when candle lit | Melody playing without flame; song looping; melody changing |
| Invisible Shortbread | Visually absent; normal texture and taste | Auditory or thermal behavior; visible distortion |
| Mushroom Hand Pie | No enchantment | Any enchantment-like behavior (flag for batch log review) |

---

## Decision Rule

**When in doubt about the level, assign the higher level and escalate.**

It is always better to quarantine and clear an item that turns out to be fine than to sell an item that escalates.
