# Enchanted Oven Temperature Guide

Quick reference for oven mode and temperature settings by product. Always verify oven status indicator before placing product inside.

---

## Oven Status Indicator Guide

| Indicator Color | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Amber** | Warming up | Wait — do not place product yet |
| **Green** | At operating temperature | Proceed with baking |
| **Blue (flashing)** | Error or charm interference | Do not use — see `common-system-errors.md` |
| **Red** | Overtemperature warning | Open Cool Down mode immediately; alert Shift Lead |
| **White** | Fully cooled / off | Safe to perform maintenance or shutdown |

---

## Temperature Settings by Product

| Product | Mode | Temperature | Bake Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Whispering Sourdough | Standard Bake | 375°E | 45 min (20 covered + 25 uncovered) | Dutch oven required; do NOT use Enchanted Mode |
| Moon-Glaze Buns | Standard Bake | 350°E | 20–25 min | Glaze applied post-bake; buns must cool before glazing |
| Dragon-Pepper Rolls | Thermal Bake | 420°E | 18–22 min | Heat-resistant gloves required; Thermal pan required |
| Singing Cake (layers) | Standard Bake | 335°E | 28–32 min | Do NOT pre-load melody stone before baking |
| Mushroom Hand Pies | Standard Bake | 390°E | 22–26 min | No special mode needed; no enchantment |
| Invisible Shortbread | Enchanted Mode | 300°E | 15–18 min | Enchanted Mode required for optical effect; do not open oven mid-bake |

---

## Mode Descriptions

| Mode | Description |
|---|---|
| **Standard Bake** | Conventional bake with no enchantment amplification. Default mode. |
| **Thermal Bake** | Elevated heat with thermal enchantment support. Required for dragon-pepper items. |
| **Enchanted Mode** | Full enchantment amplification. Use only for Invisible Shortbread. Amplifies all active charms in the oven. |
| **Cool Down** | Gradual power-down mode. Always use before full shutdown. |

---

## Common Oven Mistakes

| Mistake | Risk | Correct Action |
|---|---|---|
| Baking sourdough in Enchanted Mode | Over-amplifies auditory charm — shouting loaf | Use Standard Bake |
| Baking dragon-pepper rolls in Standard Bake | Under-bakes thermal enchantment — unstable roll | Use Thermal Bake at 420°E |
| Opening oven during Invisible Shortbread bake | Disrupts optical enchantment mid-set | Wait for timer |
| Skipping Cool Down before shutdown | Can cause charm surge | Always use Cool Down mode first |
