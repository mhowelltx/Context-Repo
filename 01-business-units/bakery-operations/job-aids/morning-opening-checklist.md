# Morning Opening Checklist

Use this checklist every morning at open. Complete in order. Initial each item.

> For full step-by-step instructions, see [`open-morning-bakery.md`](../procedures/open-morning-bakery.md).

---

## T-60 Minutes: Physical Entry

- [ ] Unlocked entrance with keycard
- [ ] Completed visual safety walkthrough (back-of-house and front counter)
- [ ] No abnormal pastry behavior observed (or: quarantined and tagged any anomalies)
- [ ] All enchantment containment bins sealed

## T-55 Minutes: System Startup

- [ ] CrumbKeeper POS powered on and logged in
- [ ] PantryLedger open on back-of-house tablet
- [ ] Order Oracle open; no Overdue or On-Hold orders (or: Shift Lead notified)

## T-50 Minutes: Ingredient Check

- [ ] PantryLedger Daily Stock Check report run
- [ ] All items at or above par level (or: Shift Lead notified of shortfalls)
- [ ] No expired stock in active bins
- [ ] Restricted ingredients authorized and retrieved (if needed for today's production)

## T-45 Minutes: Oven Startup (Shift Lead Required)

- [ ] Shift Lead present ✅
- [ ] Oven exterior inspected (no cracks, scorch marks, or charm residue)
- [ ] Runestone token inserted and ignition completed
- [ ] Oven status indicator glowing amber (warming up)
- [ ] 20-minute warm-up timer started

## T-30 Minutes: Production Prep

- [ ] Ingredients retrieved for first production batch
- [ ] Sourdough starter assessed (bubbling and whispering normally)
- [ ] Decoration station set up if singing cake orders are queued
- [ ] Batch log entries started in PantryLedger

## T-15 Minutes: Display Case and Counter

- [ ] Display case wiped down (inside and outside)
- [ ] Display items loaded with today's date labels and charm disclosure labels
- [ ] Front counter stocked: receipt rolls, tongs, apology script card, charm-disclosure cards

## T-10 Minutes: Register Opening

- [ ] CrumbKeeper: Register > Open Shift completed
- [ ] Opening float counted and matches expected amount (or: Shift Lead notified)

## T-5 Minutes: Final Check

- [ ] Shift Lead has signed off on oven startup
- [ ] All checklist items above initialed
- [ ] Front door unlocked
- [ ] Sign flipped to **OPEN**
