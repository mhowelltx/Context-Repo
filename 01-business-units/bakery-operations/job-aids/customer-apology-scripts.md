# Customer Apology Scripts

Use these scripts when responding to common customer complaints. Use the exact wording where possible. Adjust tone to match the situation — calm, sincere, and helpful.

> Do not improvise scripts for sensitive situations (safety, injury, severe allergic reaction). Escalate those immediately to the Shift Lead.

---

## General Opening (All Complaints)

> *"Thank you for letting us know. I'm sorry to hear that — let me make sure we get this sorted out for you right away."*

---

## Scenario 1: Wrong Item Received

> *"I sincerely apologize — it looks like we got your order mixed up. That's on us. Let me get the correct item for you now. If we have it ready, it'll be just a moment. If not, I can offer you a full refund or a store credit while we prepare it. Which would you prefer?"*

---

## Scenario 2: Singing Cake — Wrong Song

> *"I'm so sorry — that's not the melody you ordered, and I completely understand how disappointing that is, especially for a special occasion. We'd like to make this right. If you can leave the cake with us for a short time, our baker will reload the correct melody. Alternatively, I can process a full refund or arrange a replacement cake. What works best for you?"*

---

## Scenario 3: Enchantment Faded or Didn't Work

> *"I apologize — that shouldn't happen. Our products are made to hold their enchantment through the display period. I'd like to offer you a fresh replacement or a refund. Could you tell me when you purchased it? That will help us figure out what went wrong."*

---

## Scenario 4: Overactive Pastry Reached the Customer

> *"I am truly sorry — that's not acceptable, and I want to make sure you're okay first. Are you alright?"*
> *(Pause for response. If customer indicates any physical distress, notify Shift Lead immediately.)*
> *"I'll take care of a full refund for you right now, and I'd like our Shift Lead to speak with you as well. Please give me just a moment."*

**Do not continue the conversation alone. Call the Shift Lead now.**

---

## Scenario 5: Custom Order Not Ready at Pickup

> *"I apologize for the delay — your order isn't quite ready yet. We expect it to be complete in approximately [X] minutes. Can I offer you a complimentary tea or a sample while you wait? If the wait doesn't work for you, I'd be happy to arrange a new pickup time or a refund."*

---

## Scenario 6: Customer Upset About Price

> *"I understand — our enchanted goods do carry a premium because of the production care involved. I'm not able to adjust prices at the counter, but I'd be glad to tell you more about what goes into this item, or suggest something that fits your budget better."*

---

## Scenario 7: Dragon-Pepper Roll — Customer Sensitivity Concern

> *"I completely understand your concern. Dragon-pepper rolls contain dragon-pepper extract, which has a thermal enchantment. I can describe exactly what's in them, but I'm not able to make a personal safety guarantee. I'd suggest speaking with our Shift Lead if you have a specific sensitivity — they can provide more detail. Would you like me to get them for you?"*

---

## Closing Line (All Scenarios)

> *"Thank you again for your patience. We want every visit to Grumble & Crumb to be a good one, and I appreciate you giving us the chance to make it right."*

---

## When to Stop and Escalate

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Customer reports physical harm | Stop. Notify Shift Lead immediately. |
| Customer mentions legal action | Do not engage further. Notify Shift Lead. |
| Customer is verbally abusive | Calmly end the conversation, notify Shift Lead. |
| Refund request over 20 gold coins | Call Shift Lead to the register. |
