# Ingredient Substitution Table

Use this table when a standard ingredient is out of stock. Only use listed approved substitutes. Do not improvise substitutions not listed here.

> **Important:** If no substitute is listed or available, notify the Shift Lead. Do not proceed with production.

---

## Approved Substitutions

| Original Ingredient | Approved Substitute | Substitution Ratio | Notes | Shift Lead Approval Required? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Filtered moonwater | Filtered well-water (on-site well) | 1:1 | Slight reduction in glaze luminosity; acceptable for sourdough | No |
| Glimmer-wheat flour | Pale shimmer flour | 1:1 | Minor reduction in crust sheen; no enchantment impact | No |
| Goblin butter | Rendered troll fat (unsalted) | 1:1 | Slightly richer flavor; same baking behavior | No |
| Moon-glaze (pre-made) | Fresh moonwater reduction + moon-sugar | 2 tbsp moonwater + 1 tbsp moon-sugar per 30ml glaze | Must be made fresh; cannot be stored | Yes |
| Active sourdough starter | Emergency dry starter (supply room) | Rehydrate per package; 100g hydrated = 100g starter | Whisper enchantment may be 20% weaker for first 2 batches | Yes |
| Dragon-pepper extract | **No approved substitute** | N/A | Do not produce dragon-pepper rolls without this ingredient | N/A |
| Toadstool spores (Mushroom Pies) | Dried porcini dust | 1.5:1 (use 1.5x as much porcini) | No enchantment impact; flavor slightly earthier | No |
| Sea salt | Mineral salt (cave-harvested) | 1:1 | Do not use moon-salt as a substitute (restricted ingredient) | No |

---

## Substitution Log Requirement

All substitutions must be logged in PantryLedger under **Batch Log > Substitution Used** before production begins. Include:
- Original ingredient name
- Substitute used
- Quantity substituted
- Baker name and date

This ensures traceability and accurate allergen tracking.

---

## Substitutions That Are Never Permitted

| Do Not Substitute | Reason |
|---|---|
| Dragon-pepper extract with chili powder | Does not produce thermal enchantment; creates unsafe partial enchantment |
| Moon-salt for sea salt | Moon-salt is a restricted ingredient; use is not authorized without Shift Lead |
| Silver-based additives for any charm stabilizer | Silver disrupts enchantment; will cause charm failure |
