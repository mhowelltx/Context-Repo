# Pastry Behavior Response Table

Use this table alongside the [`pastry-behavior-severity-table.md`](../../01-business-units/bakery-operations/job-aids/pastry-behavior-severity-table.md) to determine the correct response to each level of overactive pastry event.

---

## Response Table by Level

| Level | Behavior Examples | Touch Item? | Clear Area? | Notify Shift Lead? | Order Oracle Log? | Sell Item? | Dispose? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Louder whisper, minor extra glow | Yes — with tongs | No | Within 15 min | Yes | Shift Lead discretion | Only if Shift Lead directs |
| **2** | Pulsing light, crumb surge, mild warmth | Yes — with tongs | Optional | Yes — promptly | Yes | No — quarantine first | Only after Shift Lead clearance |
| **3** | Sparks, looping melody, elevated heat | **No** | **Yes** | **Immediately** | Yes | **No** | After Shift Lead approval |
| **4** | Open flame, structural damage, sustained levitation | **No** | **Evacuate** | **Immediately** | Yes | **No** | After Shift Lead + Manager approval |

---

## Response Steps by Level

### Level 1
1. Use tongs to move item to cooling rack quarantine area.
2. Tag the item: item name, time, behavior, your name.
3. Monitor for 60 minutes.
4. Notify Shift Lead within 15 minutes.
5. Log in Order Oracle.
6. Shift Lead determines whether to clear for sale or dispose.

### Level 2
1. Use tongs (or charm-safe paddle) to move item to cooling rack quarantine area.
2. Tag the item.
3. Notify Shift Lead promptly.
4. Log in Order Oracle.
5. Do not return item to display without Shift Lead clearance.

### Level 3
1. Do not touch. Step back.
2. Clear all people from the immediate area.
3. Notify Shift Lead immediately — call out verbally or by phone.
4. Do not re-enter the area until Shift Lead arrives.
5. Shift Lead assesses and directs next steps.
6. Log full incident in Order Oracle.
7. Dispose of item after Shift Lead approval.

### Level 4
1. Do not touch. Call out "CLEAR" loudly.
2. Evacuate all people from the area (back-of-house and/or bakery).
3. Shift Lead contacts Bakery Manager immediately.
4. Follow emergency procedures if fire or structural risk is present.
5. Do not re-enter until cleared by Shift Lead.
6. Complete Order Oracle incident report as soon as area is safe.

---

## Post-Incident Checklist

After any overactive pastry event (all levels):

- [ ] Incident logged in Order Oracle with all required fields
- [ ] Item quarantine tag number recorded in Order Oracle ticket
- [ ] Area cleared of charm residue using charm-clearing solution
- [ ] Batch log updated with incident notation
- [ ] Shift Lead sign-off obtained on incident record
- [ ] If customer affected: refund processed and Level 2+ escalation policy applied
