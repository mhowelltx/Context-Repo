# Guest Service Principles

These principles govern all customer interactions at Grumble & Crumb Goblin Bakery. Every associate is responsible for applying them in every interaction.

---

## The Five Principles

### 1. Acknowledge First

Every customer who enters the bakery deserves to be acknowledged within 30 seconds, even if you are busy. A nod, a greeting, or a brief "I'll be right with you" is sufficient.

### 2. Listen Completely

Before responding to a complaint or question, let the customer finish speaking. Do not interrupt. Demonstrate that you are listening by making eye contact and nodding. Repeat back the key issue before responding.

### 3. Take Ownership

When a customer has a problem, it is your problem to solve — even if you did not cause it. Do not say "that's not my department" or "I don't know." Either solve it or find someone who can.

### 4. Set Clear Expectations

If you cannot resolve something immediately, tell the customer:
- What you are going to do.
- How long it will take.
- What they can expect.

Do not make promises you cannot keep.

### 5. Close with Care

Every interaction ends with a genuine close. Thank the customer. Check that their concern has been addressed. Invite them back. Use the closing script from [`customer-apology-scripts.md`](../../01-business-units/bakery-operations/job-aids/customer-apology-scripts.md) when appropriate.

---

## Service Recovery Framework

When something goes wrong, use this four-step model:

1. **Apologize** — Sincerely, without excuses.
2. **Understand** — Ask one or two questions to clarify the issue.
3. **Resolve** — Offer a concrete solution (remake, refund, future discount).
4. **Follow Through** — Do what you said you would do.

---

## What We Do Not Do

- We do not argue with customers.
- We do not tell customers they are wrong about their experience.
- We do not make personal safety guarantees about enchanted products.
- We do not offer compensation beyond our approved authority without Shift Lead approval.
- We do not discuss other customers' orders or complaints.
