# Bakery and Magic Terms Reference

This document supplements the main glossary with extended definitions and context for bakery-specific and enchantment-related terms. For core definitions, see [`glossary.md`](../../00-start-here/glossary.md).

---

## Baking Terms

**Autolyse** — The rest period after mixing flour and water, before adding starter and salt. Develops gluten passively and improves dough extensibility.

**Bulk Fermentation** — The primary leavening period after mixing, during which the dough grows and develops flavor.

**Crumb Coat** — A thin first layer of frosting applied to a cake to seal in crumbs before the final decorative frosting layer.

**Dutch Oven** — A covered cast-iron baking vessel used for sourdough. Traps steam during the initial bake for optimal crust development.

**FIFO** — First In, First Out. Stock rotation rule: the oldest stock is used before newer stock.

**Par Level** — The minimum on-hand quantity of an ingredient that triggers a restock. Set in PantryLedger for each ingredient.

**Proofing** — The final rise period for shaped dough before baking.

**Stretch-and-Fold** — A technique used during bulk fermentation to develop dough structure without traditional kneading.

## Magic and Enchantment Terms

**Auditory Enchantment** — An enchantment that produces sound. Whispering sourdough and singing cakes carry auditory enchantments.

**Charm Amplification** — An increase in enchantment intensity beyond intended parameters. Can be caused by ingredient over-use, conflicting enchantments, or storage near similar enchantment types.

**Charm Contact** — The moment an enchantment activates upon a triggering stimulus (heat, light, sound, or touch).

**Charm Dampening** — Reducing or neutralizing an active enchantment, typically by applying charm-clearing solution or using an enchantment-dampening cloth.

**Charm Discharge** — An uncontrolled release of enchantment energy, often caused by silver contact or conflicting charm types.

**Charm Socket** — The small mount on a singing cake base into which the melody stone is inserted.

**Enchantment-Dampening Cloth** — A cloth treated to suppress active enchantments during proofing or transport. Required for whispering sourdough proofing.

**Enchantment-Stable Container** — A storage container that does not interact with or amplify the enchantments of its contents.

**Goblin-Safe Handling** — Handling protocols adapted for goblin physiology: heat-tolerant hands, sensitivity to silver, preference for dim lighting during rest periods.

**Luminescent Enchantment** — An enchantment that produces light. Moon-glaze buns carry luminescent enchantments.

**Melody Stone** — A small enchanted stone pre-loaded with a specific musical melody. Inserted into a singing cake to provide the auditory enchantment.

**Optical Enchantment** — An enchantment that affects the appearance or visibility of an item. Invisible shortbread carries an optical enchantment.

**Runestone Token** — The ignition token for the enchanted oven. Controls the oven's enchantment mode. Stored in the Shift Lead's lockbox.

**Thermal Enchantment** — An enchantment that produces or retains heat. Dragon-pepper rolls carry a thermal enchantment.

## System Terms

**Authorization PIN** — The Shift Lead's personal PIN used in PantryLedger to authorize restricted ingredient access or in CrumbKeeper to approve large refunds.

**Closing Report** — The end-of-day financial report generated by CrumbKeeper when the register is closed.

**Incident Ticket** — An Order Oracle record documenting a product defect, safety event, or customer complaint.

**Lot Number** — The identifier printed on ingredient packages indicating the batch of origin. Entered in PantryLedger at time of receipt.

**Opening Float** — The starting cash amount in the register at the beginning of each shift.

**Production Queue** — The list of items to be baked on a given day, driven by custom orders and standard production targets.
