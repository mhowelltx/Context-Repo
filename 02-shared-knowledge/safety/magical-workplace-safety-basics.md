# Magical Workplace Safety Basics

All associates working in an enchanted food production environment must follow these baseline safety practices. These apply in addition to any product-specific safety requirements in the relevant procedure.

---

## Personal Protective Equipment

| Situation | Required PPE |
|---|---|
| Handling dragon-pepper extract | Heat-resistant gloves |
| Working near the enchanted oven (Thermal Bake mode) | Heat-resistant gloves and closed-toe shoes |
| Clearing charm residue from surfaces | Standard food-safe gloves |
| Handling invisible shortbread (storage) | Standard gloves — use tongs to confirm location |
| Responding to Level 2+ overactive pastry | Do not touch; notify Shift Lead |

---

## General Safety Rules

1. **Never leave an enchanted item unattended** in an active state on a prep surface.
2. **No silver tools or containers** in the production area — silver disrupts charm stability and can cause discharge.
3. **Clear charm residue** between batches of different enchantment types. Use only the approved charm-clearing solution.
4. **Do not store conflicting enchantment types together** (e.g., auditory items with thermal items).
5. **Know your Level** — assess all overactive pastry events using the severity table before acting.
6. **The first aid kit** is located under the front counter. All associates must know its location.
7. **Emergency exits** are at the front entrance and the back-of-house rear door. Keep both clear at all times.

---

## Enchantment Exposure Risks

| Enchantment Type | Risk if Mishandled | Symptom |
|---|---|---|
| Thermal (dragon-pepper) | Burn injury | Pain, redness on skin contact |
| Auditory (sourdough, singing cake) | Auditory discomfort from amplified charm | Ringing, temporary sensitivity to sound |
| Luminescent (moon-glaze) | Eye discomfort from prolonged exposure | Spots in vision, temporary |
| Optical (invisible shortbread) | Trip hazard if not contained | N/A — containment prevents exposure |

If any associate experiences a symptom above, remove them from the area, apply first aid if appropriate, and notify the Shift Lead.

---

## Emergency Procedures

| Situation | Immediate Action |
|---|---|
| Burn from thermal item | Cool water for 10 minutes; first aid kit; notify Shift Lead |
| Level 4 overactive pastry | Evacuate area; Shift Lead calls Bakery Manager |
| Charm residue on skin | Rinse with clean water; notify Shift Lead |
| Fire | Use the standard extinguisher (NOT the charm-clearing solution); evacuate; call emergency services |
| Associate injured | First aid kit; notify Shift Lead; document in Order Oracle |

---

## Reporting

All safety incidents — no matter how minor — must be reported to the Shift Lead and logged in Order Oracle on the day they occur.
