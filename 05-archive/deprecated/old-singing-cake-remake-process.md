# [DEPRECATED] Old Singing Cake Remake Process

**Deprecated Date:** 2025-10-01
**Reason for Deprecation:** The melody stone loading system was redesigned in Q4 2025. The prior process required manual stone coding by the Shift Lead, which was error-prone and caused the majority of wrong-melody complaints. The new process uses pre-coded stones from the melody cabinet indexed to song codes in Order Oracle.

---

## What This Document Covered (Historical Reference Only)

Prior to October 2025, remaking a singing cake with a wrong melody required:

1. Returning the entire cake to the baker for full redecoration.
2. The Shift Lead manually coding a new melody stone using the Melody Encoding Device (MED).
3. A 4–6 hour remake window, which often required the customer to return the next day.
4. No standard script existed for this complaint; associates improvised responses.

### Why This Process Failed

- Manual melody encoding was inconsistent. Bakers with less experience often loaded the wrong melody despite re-coding.
- The 4–6 hour window was unacceptable for same-day celebration orders.
- No logging requirement meant wrong-melody incidents went untracked.

---

## Replaced By

The current process uses pre-coded melody stones indexed in Order Oracle. Stone swaps take under 20 minutes. Standard apology scripts are defined in [`customer-apology-scripts.md`](../../01-business-units/bakery-operations/job-aids/customer-apology-scripts.md).
