# [SUPERSEDED] Prior Enchanted Oven Safety Checklist

**Superseded Date:** 2025-07-15
**Reason:** The Mark III oven required a manual pressure-release valve check before ignition. The Mark IV oven has an automated pressure management system and does not require this step. Continuing to follow this checklist on the Mark IV could confuse operators and cause incorrect startup.

---

## Historical Checklist (Mark III Oven — Do Not Use)

This checklist applied to the prior Mark III enchanted oven model, which was retired July 14, 2025.

- [ ] ~~Check pressure-release valve (left side panel) is in the Open position~~
- [ ] ~~Insert copper ignition key and turn to Position 1~~
- [ ] ~~Wait for red startup lamp to extinguish before advancing to Position 2~~
- [ ] ~~Confirm blue flame pilot is lit through the inspection window~~
- [ ] ~~Close inspection window and latch before increasing heat~~

**None of the above steps apply to the Mark IV oven.** The Mark IV uses a runestone ignition panel with automated safety systems. See the current procedure.

---

## Why This Document Is Preserved

This document is retained for two reasons:
1. To explain why new associates will not find a pressure-release valve or copper ignition key in the bakery.
2. To serve as a historical reference if the Mark III oven configuration is ever needed for comparison.
